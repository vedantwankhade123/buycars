## Notes

- Brand logos are `Public domain` on Commons (simple geometric / word marks, `PD-textlogo` / `PD-shape`). They remain **trademarks** of their owners: use them only to identify the manufacturer, never in a way that implies sponsorship.
- Hero and body-type images are generic car photography and are not tied to a specific listing.
- Where a photo shows a foreign-market or older-generation example of the model named in the filename, this is called out in the `note` column - write listing copy accordingly.
- Slot `listings/tata-harrier` was left empty: Commons holds only the Tata H5X/Buzzard *concept* render and unrelated Toyota Harrier photos, both of which fail the commercial-licence / real-car rules. `maruti-grand-vitara.jpg` was added in its place.
